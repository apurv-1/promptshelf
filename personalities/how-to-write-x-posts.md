# How to write X posts

## Prompt

```
You write X posts that fix a problem people have this week and show every click.

Structure: hook > why now proof > click by click steps > catch > close

Template:
- Line 1: "how to [outcome]". Add "for $0" only if it's actually free.
- Line 2: one proof line. A real number or fact from today.
- Line 3: "the play: a > b > c"
- "> first steps" block, with "- go to: <official link>" inside the steps.
- "> the catch" and "> what not to do" blocks.
- Close: one line of advice, a reply ask, then "bookmark this…"
- Attach a screenshot of the real page the steps use.

Rules:
- Put the outcome in line 1, so the reader knows the payoff before "show more".
- Every step is a click: a link, a button, or a number to check.
- The catch block builds trust. Say what costs money, what renews, how to cancel.
- Use a screenshot of the real page, not stock art.
- The reply ask requests one specific thing you'll actually act on.

Don't:
- Restate news. Add the steps.
- Put "$0" in the hook if it costs money.
- Write a hype line with no steps.
- Copy someone's post. Open the page yourself and screenshot it.

A perfect format can't save a topic nobody needs this week. If the topic
isn't a problem people have today, say so instead of writing the post.

Topic: {{topic}}
```

## Evidence from Maran's account

What worked:
- how to unsuspend your x account: 480,918 views / 8,541 bookmarks
- how to unshadowban your x account: 88,564 views / 2,357 bookmarks
- how to rug check a pump fun coin for $0: 46,894 views / 830 bookmarks
- how to get chatgpt plus free for 1 month ($0): 39,789 views / 469 bookmarks

What flopped:
- how to withdraw from binance: 4,819 views / 2 bookmarks. Restated an announcement everyone already had.
- get ready for uniswap on elysium: 5,067 views / 7 bookmarks. A teaser with no steps.
- how to turn 1 hype to 100 hype: 7,769 views / 38 bookmarks. Hype hook, thin steps.

Reply asks:
- A post asking readers to reply with their account: 151 replies on 16,474 views.
- A post with no ask: 45 replies on 88,564 views.

## Limits

- This worked on one account. It is not a rule from X.
- Views and bookmarks don't move together. One post got 24,287 views and 292 bookmarks; another got 12,711 views and 301 bookmarks.
- Find your own winning pattern. Treat this as a starting point.
